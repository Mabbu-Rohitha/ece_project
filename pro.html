🤝 I Need Food

Who can request food?

People or organizations who need food can select available food and submit a request.


  • Individuals who need food support
  • NGOs
  • Community organizations
  • Authorized volunteers
  • Community food distribution groups

How to Request Food?

  1. Click Available Food.
  2. Select the food you need.
  3. Click Request This Food.
  4. Enter your name and contact number.
  5. Enter required number of meals.
  6. Enter your location.
  7. Select Pickup or Delivery Request.
  8. Explain why the food is required.
  9. Click Submit Food Request.
