🤝 Need Food?

Who can request food?

People or organizations who need food can use FoodShare to check available food and submit a request.

Examples:

  • NGOs
  • Community organizations
  • Food relief groups
  • Authorized volunteers

How to Request Food?

  1. Go to Available Food.
  2. Select the food you need.
  3. Click Request Food.
  4. Enter your name and contact details.
  5. Enter required number of meals.
  6. Enter your receiving location.
  7. Select Pickup or Delivery Request.
  8. Submit the request.
  9. Track your request from the Requests section.
